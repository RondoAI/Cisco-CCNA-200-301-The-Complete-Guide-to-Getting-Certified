# References & Inspiration

Context for anyone (or any agent) picking up this repo.

## UI / capability inspiration

The visual ambition — a live globe/map, a self-explaining situation feed, a
readable timeline, instant legibility of a complex picture — is inspired by
Palantir Gotham demos:

- https://youtu.be/YDAxITCNcko
- https://youtu.be/VB_6oi0g_f8
- https://youtu.be/xgQXfzUgTO4

(Plus two Gotham product screenshots reviewed during design.)

## The boundary (important — see CHARTER.md)

Those references are Palantir's *defense* product: operational course-of-action
planning, force employment, and ISR targeting (e.g., identifying a vessel's
weapons loadout to support a strike).

**We take the interface ambition, not the function.** Glasshouse builds
situational awareness and accountability — understanding and sourcing what is
happening. It does **not** build targeting, force-employment planning, weapons
identification for engagement, ISR that supports a kill chain, or any operational
military capability. We may *identify and cite* a platform or weapon from open
sources ("reported as X, see public references"); we never operationalize it.

These references are recorded for design context only. Their content is not
copied into this repository.
